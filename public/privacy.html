Room for privacy.

This notice covers the crescentbrowser.com website, optional Crescent Cloud accounts, and the crash reports and usage analytics that Crescent Mobile for iOS sends.

This website

This website uses locally bundled fonts, images, and scripts. It includes no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, newsletter form, or browser storage, and it has no sign-in of its own.

Interactive examples

The website reads a same-origin static releases.json file to show available installers. It does not send operating-system information to a separate service. Clicking a download opens a GitHub-hosted release asset. The phone, Profile carousel, import illustration, and connection choices use synthetic content and temporary page state. They do not sign in, connect devices, read another browser, submit information, or save your selections.

External links

Links to GitHub, brand owners, and source collections open separate services with their own privacy policies. This website does not collect email addresses or send form submissions.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of hosting request metadata. This site does not add analytics or tracking scripts.

Crescent Cloud accounts

Crescent Cloud accounts are optional and run separately at accounts.crescentbrowser.com. You never need one to browse, import from another browser, or sync with a Crescent Hub you host yourself. If you create one, we store:

  • your name, email address, optional username, and whether your email is confirmed;
  • your password as a one-way hash, if you use a password. We never see or store the password itself;
  • if you sign in with Google or Microsoft: that provider’s account ID and the sign-in tokens it issues. We request only your name, email address and basic profile, and don’t use the tokens to access anything else;
  • for each signed-in browser or device: a session with its IP address, browser or app description, and expiry;
  • short-lived records for email confirmation, password reset, device sign-in codes, and per-IP rate limits that protect against abuse.

We use this information only to sign you in, keep your account secure, and send account emails such as email confirmation and password reset. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising or tracking, and we don’t combine it with the crash reports and usage analytics described below.

Crescent Cloud runs on Cloudflare, which stores the account database in its Asia-Pacific region and processes request metadata, including IP addresses, to run and protect the service. Account emails are delivered by Sender.net. If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, their privacy policies apply to that sign-in.

Account information is kept while your account exists. Sessions expire after 30 days without use, and confirmation, reset and device codes expire within minutes to hours. To delete your account and its sign-in data, email admin@crescentbrowser.com from the address on the account. See also our Terms of Service.

Crash reports and usage analytics in Crescent Mobile

Crescent Mobile for iOS sends two kinds of information to Crescent, so we can find and fix crashes and bugs and see which features are used:

  • crash and performance data: crash reports and performance measurements, such as launch time and moments when the app stops responding, used for crash reporting and debugging;
  • usage analytics: which features of the app are used.

This information is not linked to your name, email address or Crescent Cloud account. We don’t use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites or for advertising, and we don’t sell it. It does not include the addresses or content of the pages you visit, or your browsing or search history.

Crescent Browser and Crescent Hub

Product-specific privacy information accompanies Crescent Browser, Crescent Mobile, and Crescent Hub releases. The crash reports and usage analytics above are those of Crescent Mobile for iOS; other Crescent apps say in their own release information whether they send any. Cookies and site storage never sync.
