Room for privacy.

This landing page uses locally bundled fonts and images. It includes no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, account system, newsletter form, or browser storage.

External links

Links to GitHub take you to a separate service with its own privacy policy. This website does not send any form submissions or collect email addresses.

Hosting

This repository does not include a production deployment. A future hosting provider may process ordinary request metadata, such as IP addresses and access logs. The hosting details and retention policy should be added here before publication.

The browser and personal Hub

This page describes the website only. Crescent Browser and Crescent Hub are still under development and will need their own release privacy and security documentation. Cookies and site storage are excluded from the planned cross-device continuity system.
