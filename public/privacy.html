Room for privacy.

This notice covers the crescentbrowser.com website, optional Crescent Cloud accounts, and what Crescent Mobile for iOS sends to Crescent.

This website

This website uses locally bundled fonts, images, and scripts. It includes no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, cookies, newsletter form, or browser storage, and it has no sign-in of its own.

Interactive examples

The website reads a same-origin static releases.json file to show available installers. It does not send operating-system information to a separate service. Clicking a download opens a GitHub-hosted release asset. The phone, Profile carousel, import illustration, and connection choices use synthetic content and temporary page state. They do not sign in, connect devices, read another browser, submit information, or save your selections.

External links

Links to GitHub, brand owners, and source collections open separate services with their own privacy policies. This website does not collect email addresses or send form submissions.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its handling of hosting request metadata. This site does not add analytics or tracking scripts.

Crescent Cloud accounts

Crescent Cloud accounts are optional and run separately at accounts.crescentbrowser.com. You never need one to browse, import from another browser, or sync with a Crescent Hub you host yourself. You can sign in with Apple, Google, Microsoft, or an email address and password. If you create an account, we store:

  • your name, email address, optional username, and whether your email is confirmed;
  • if you use a password: only a one-way hash of it, never the password itself;
  • if you sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft: that provider’s account ID and the sign-in tokens it issues. We request only your name and email address (and, from Google and Microsoft, your basic profile), and we don’t use the tokens to access anything else. With Sign in with Apple you can hide your email address: we then receive an Apple relay address that forwards our account emails to you;
  • for each signed-in browser or device: a session with its IP address, browser or app description, and expiry;
  • when a signed-in phone asks to join the phone sync of a computer signed in to the same account: the phone’s name as iOS gives it to apps (usually just “iPhone”) and its platform, and the one-time pairing code that computer sends back. The code holds the computer’s sync public key, a one-time secret, the sync vault ID, and the private-network or Tailscale addresses where the phone can reach the computer;
  • short-lived records for email confirmation, password reset, device sign-in codes, and per-IP rate limits that protect against abuse.

We use this information only to sign you in, keep your account secure, link your own devices, and send account emails such as email confirmation and password reset. We don’t sell it or use it for advertising or tracking, and we don’t combine it with any crash reports or usage analytics.

Because the same account means the same person, a phone signed in to your account joins your computer’s phone sync without a QR code. Anyone who controls your account could therefore add a phone; Crescent Browser on your computer shows which phone joined, with Remove.

Crescent Cloud runs on Cloudflare, which stores the account database in its Asia-Pacific region and processes request metadata, including IP addresses, to run and protect the service. Account emails are delivered by Sender.net. If you sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft, their privacy policies apply to that sign-in.

Account information is kept while your account exists. Sessions expire after 30 days without use. Confirmation, reset and device codes expire within minutes to hours, and link requests, with their pairing codes, within twenty minutes. A daily cleanup deletes whatever has expired.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in Crescent Mobile (Settings, your account, Delete Account) or on accounts.crescentbrowser.com (Delete account). This permanently removes the account, its sign-in methods, every signed-in session and any open link requests. If you used Sign in with Apple, we also remove Crescent Browser from your Apple account. Browsing data on your devices isn’t affected. You can also email admin@crescentbrowser.com from the address on the account. See also our Terms of Service.

Crescent Mobile for iOS

Crescent Mobile works without an account. Tap Not now on the welcome screen to browse and keep Spaces on your iPhone without signing in.

If you sign in, the app opens accounts.crescentbrowser.com in an in-app Safari view, so the app itself never sees your password. It keeps the session token in the iOS Keychain, on that iPhone only. It sends Crescent Cloud only the sign-in request and then that token, a session check that returns your name and email address for Settings, and, until the iPhone is paired with a computer, the link request described above. Signing out ends that iPhone’s session.

Crescent Mobile does not send crash reports, usage analytics, page addresses, page content, or your browsing or search history to Crescent, and it doesn’t track you. Pages and searches go to the sites and the search engine you choose, as in any browser. Phone sync runs directly between your iPhone and your computer, over the same Wi-Fi or your own Tailscale network; it is encrypted on the devices, and its keys never go to Crescent Cloud.

A later version may offer crash and performance reports and usage analytics. If it does, each will be off until you turn it on, will not be linked to your name, email address or Crescent Cloud account, will not be used to track you or for advertising, and will never include the addresses or content of the pages you visit, or your browsing or search history. This notice will describe them before that version is released.
